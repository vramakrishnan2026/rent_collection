Privacy

Challaghatta Rent is a private app used only by the owner of Challaghatta Home and the building’s security guard. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google sign-in is used only to check that you are on the approved list. The app keeps your email address and first name in a signed cookie on your device.

Google Drive: if the owner links Drive, tenant ID photos are saved to a folder the app creates in the owner’s own Drive. The app can only see files it created. ID numbers are never read or stored.

Tenant records (names, phone numbers, rent and payments) are kept in the owner’s Cloudflare account and are never shared or sold.

Payments are handled by Razorpay under Razorpay’s own privacy policy.
